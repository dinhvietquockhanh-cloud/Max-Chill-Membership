name: Max Chill membership pilot
description: Pilot size and approval status for Max Chill Garden's membership program.
---

The first rollout targets the first 100 Max Chill Garden guests. Membership tiers and their benefits are proposals for the pilot, not approved policy; the owner will review them.

**Why:** This is the business scope and approval boundary stated in the project brief.

**How to apply:** Keep tier terms clearly labeled as proposed and do not present or grant their benefits as confirmed policy until the owner approves them.
